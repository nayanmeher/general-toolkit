General Toolkit Privacy Policy

Effective 18 September 2026 · Applies to the General Toolkit app for Android and iOS

In short

  • Your images are processed on your device and are never uploaded.
  • The app collects no personal data: no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking.
  • Anything the app stores stays on your device, and you can delete it at any time.

1. Who we are

General Toolkit is developed and published by Nayan Meher, an individual developer based in India ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles your information. Contact details are in section 11.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or receive any personal data through the app. In particular:

  • There is no account or sign-in.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
  • The app does not connect to the internet to process your images or to send us anything.

The only network activity involves purchases, which Google Play handles (section 7), and opening this policy in your web browser when you choose to.

3. Your images

  • Compressing, resizing, converting, cropping and creating PDFs all happen on your device.
  • Your original images are never changed. The app always writes a new file.
  • Location (GPS) and camera details are removed from every image the app creates, so they are not passed on when you share the result.

4. Permissions

  • Photos: used only when you choose images to work on or save a result to your gallery. The app does not read your photo library in the background.
  • The app does not use your camera, microphone, location or contacts.

You can change or withdraw photo access at any time in your device's settings.

5. Information stored on your device

To work, the app keeps a small amount of information on your device only. None of it is sent to us.

  • History: a list of your recent results (file names, sizes and dates) and one copy of each result you saved or shared, so you can share it again.
  • Settings: your theme, language and default quality and format.
  • Daily usage count: on the free plan, how many images and PDFs you have saved or shared today, used to apply the daily limit. It resets each day.
  • Working files: temporary files created while a tool is open, deleted when you leave it.

To delete this information, use Clear history in the app's settings, or uninstall the app, which removes everything the app has stored.

6. Device backups

Your device's own backup service may include the app's settings and history list. On Android, the image copies kept for history are excluded from backups. On iPhone and iPad, backups may include them. Backups are made and stored by Google or Apple under their privacy policies, not by us.

7. Purchases

General Toolkit Pro is sold through Google Play (and, in future, the Apple App Store). The store handles payment. We never see your card, UPI or other payment details. The app only receives confirmation from the store, on your device, that you own Pro, so it can unlock Pro features.

The store may share limited transaction information with us, such as an order number, the product, the price and the country of purchase. We use it only for refunds, support and legally required tax records. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

8. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal data, because we do not collect any. When you use the app's Share button, the file goes only to the app or person you choose, through your device's share sheet.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children, and it collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

10. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Everything the app stores is on your device and under your control, as described in sections 5 and 6. You can still contact us with any question or complaint about privacy, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

11. Contact and grievances

Grievance officer: Nayan Meher
Email: dev.app.nayan@gmail.com

We aim to reply within 7 days, and to resolve any complaint within 30 days.

12. Changes to this policy

If how the app handles information changes, for example if a feature ever needs an internet connection, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the app. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
